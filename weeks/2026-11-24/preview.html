Thanksgiving Day and Beyond: 5 Non-Alcoholic Beers to Pour

Thanksgiving is Thursday, and it's a long day at the table: snacks while the turkey roasts, the big meal, football, pie, and leftovers that somehow taste better on Friday. Every stretch of it deserves a good beer.

Whatever your reason for pouring NA this year, here are five picks to carry you from the first appetizer to the last game of the weekend. Each one is a single can, so mix them into a Build Your Own 12-Pack and set out something for everyone.

Bulltown Brewing Bulltown Blonde

Blonde, Lagers & Lights · $4.99 a can

Bulltown Blonde is the flagship from Bulltown Brewing, a crew of Midwestern dads who believe beer should taste like beer. It's crisp and fuller-bodied than you'd expect from a blonde, the kind of easy, familiar pour that works for the relatives who just want a beer. Set a few out with the main course.

1820 Brewing Wave Finder IPA

IPA · $4.49 a can

Wave Finder comes from 1820 Brewing in Portland, Maine, built on Mosaic, Cascade, El Dorado and Citra hops over malted barley and wheat. Expect bright citrus, real hop bitterness and a crisp, clean finish. An IPA's bite stands up to rich sides like stuffing and gravy, and it's a natural for the afternoon football game.

Bulltown's Grapefruit Radler pairs gentle sweetness with a zesty grapefruit tang and a smooth finish. It's a lighter, brighter pour that's great with the appetizer spread, or as a refresher after the big plate. We're down to the last few cans, so grab yours soon.

Athletic Brewing Aftershift Zesty Session

Athletic Brewing made Aftershift Zesty Session with chefs including Chris Cosentino, and it shows: a golden, light-bodied beer with vibrant lime leaf and yuzu, a touch of tartness and a clean finish. It's made for the cook, the one to open once the dishes are done. Only a handful of cans are left.
