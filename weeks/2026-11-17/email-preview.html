Thanksgiving Table: 5 Non-Alcoholic Beers Worth Pouring

A bone-dry West Coast pils, a rare small-batch Mexican lager, a balanced ale and two juicy IPAs. Five NA beers for the Thanksgiving table, plus build your own 12-pack.

Beaglepuss Dry Curious West Coast Pilz

Lagers & Lights, Pilsner

Beaglepuss takes a classic pilsner and gives it a West Coast hop twist: lean, bone-dry and crisp, with bursts of citrus and pine and a hint of dank resin over clean pilsner malt. That dry, snappy finish is exactly what you want next to buttery mashed potatoes and salty gravy. Pour it with the main course.

Shop this beer · $5.99

Switchback NA Ale

Pale Ale

Switchback NA Ale is all about balance: a bright hop aroma, subtle fruity notes from the brewery's own house yeast and a smooth malt backbone. It's brewed with the same care as the rest of Switchback's lineup, using the BrewVo system. An easygoing pour that works with everything on the plate, from turkey to the leftover sandwich the next day.

Shop this beer · $4.49

Best Day Endless Mango IPA

Fruity IPA, IPA & Hoppy

Best Day keeps this one simple with just five ingredients: water, barley, hops, yeast and real mango. It's sweet, a little tart and bright with citrus, with a crisp, refreshing finish. Open one while the turkey's in the oven or set it out with the appetizers.

Shop this beer · $4.49

Switchback Brewing Krush NA IPA

IPA, IPA & Hoppy

Krush is Switchback's full-bodied hazy NA IPA, made in collaboration with Yakima Chief Hops and Sustainable Beverage Technologies. Expect a tropical aroma with layers of orange, citrus and exotic fruit, and a smooth body that stays easy to drink. It's the one to crack when the Thanksgiving football game kicks off.

Shop this beer · $4.99

BrewVo & New Terrain Brewing Maintain Mexican Brew

Cerveza, Lager

This Mexican-style lager is a rare find: BrewVo and New Terrain brewed it in small batches for the 2026 Great American Beer Festival, aiming for gold. Corn and a touch of Vienna malt keep it crisp and crushable, and a Motueka and Abstrax dry hop adds a bright lift. Small-batch cans don't last, so grab a couple for the table while they're here.

Shop this beer · $4.49
