Dark and Malty: 4 Non-Alcoholic Beers for Cold November Nights

A Märzen, a roasty stout, a lime-kissed amber and a real-pumpkin fall brew. Four darker NA beers for cold nights, plus build your own 12-pack.

Best Day Oktoberfest

Marzen, Oktoberfest

A classic Märzen-style Festbier with a warm amber glow, malty aroma and a subtly sweet body balanced by a light hop touch, finishing smooth and crisp. Best Day brews this one as a limited-time seasonal, so grab it while the season lasts. It's a natural fit next to bratwurst, pretzels or a weekend of football.

Shop this beer · $4.49

1820 Brewing Stand Up & Stout

Dark

1820 Brewing builds this stout on malted barley, roasted barley, malted wheat and rolled oats, with Nugget, East Kent Golding and Fuggles hops for earthy, lightly bitter notes. It's medium-bodied with a mild sweetness and a smooth roasted finish, rich without being heavy. Pour one with dessert or by the fire on a cold night.

Shop this beer · $3.99

Ceebo Mexican Style Amber with Lime

Amber

Ceebo's amber cerveza brings dark roasted malt and toffee notes, brightened by a hint of lime. It's a session-friendly way into darker beers if you usually reach for something lighter. Line it up with tacos or anything off the grill.

Shop this beer · $4.99

Go Brewing F*k'in Pum'kin

Pumpkin

Go Brewing skips the gimmicks here: real pumpkin purée with cinnamon, nutmeg, clove, allspice and ginger for a smooth, spiced fall beer. It's a limited-edition seasonal, so it won't be around forever. Save one for pumpkin pie or the Thanksgiving run-up.

Shop this beer · $4.49
